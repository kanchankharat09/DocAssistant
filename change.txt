STEP 0. Add the files (this is the only real work)
--------------------------------------------------
For each of the 8 cases, put 2 files in the test_data folder:
    T06_transcript.txt
    T06_expected_summary.txt
The file names must follow exactly this pattern, otherwise the scripts will
not find them.

Rules (same as T02 and T04):
  - The wrong medicine name or dose goes inside the transcript, in the
    doctor's prescription line.
  - The expected summary has the correct value.
  - The expected summary keeps the same top lines as the other summaries:
        Patient: Name, 58M
        Date: 15 Oct 2026
        Chief complaint: ...
        Medicines prescribed:
        - medicine line
        Doctor instructions: ...
    (seed.py reads these exact words, so do not rename them.)
