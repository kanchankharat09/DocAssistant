PROJECT NOTES (Hindi-English): Patient History Assistant
=========================================================
Ye notes aap kisi ko project samjhane ke liye use kar sakte ho.
Numbers 29 Oct 2026 tak ke final run ke hain (S1 se S12 done).


1. PROJECT EK MINUTE MEIN (ye aap bol sakte ho)
------------------------------------------------
Ek chhote clinic mein doctor patient se baat karta hai. Us baat-cheet ka likha hua
transcript hum AI ko dete hain. AI usse ek chhota structured summary banata hai:
complaint, kitne din se, purani history, aur medicines with dose.

Doctor summary padhta hai, kuch galat ho to theek karta hai, phir Approve dabata hai.
Approve ke baad hi summary save hoti hai.

Agle visit par doctor patient ko search karta hai aur ek "recall card" dekhta hai:
pichla visit kuch seconds mein, poora transcript padhne ki zarurat nahi.

AI aur doctor ke beech ek checker hai. Agar medicine ka naam ya dose galat lage to
checker flag laga deta hai, taaki galti galti se save na ho jaye.


2. HUM YE PROJECT KYUN BANA RAHE HAIN
-------------------------------------
Repeat patients bahut aate hain aur doctor ke paas time kam hota hai.
Hum 2 sawaal ka jawab jaanna chahte hain:
  (1) Kya AI ka summary sahi aur safe hai?
  (2) Kya recall card transcript padhne se sach mein tez hai?

Ye sab MOCK data par test hai: 20 banaye hue transcripts, 20 banaye hue patients.
Koi real patient nahi, koi real recording nahi.

Hypothesis (humara idea, jo hum test karte hain):
  "20 transcripts mein se kam se kam 18 summaries mein har zaruri detail rahe, aur
   role-play karne wale doctors card se key details median 15 second se kam mein
   dhoondh lein, aur ye transcript padhne se kam se kam 2 guna tez ho."
Hum galat hain agar: score kam hai, ya koi galat dose bina flag ke save ho jaye, ya
card 2 guna tez nahi hai.

7. JO IDEAS AAP KO SAMJHANI HAIN
--------------------------------
- Prompt: wo likhi hui instruction jo hum AI ko dete hain. run1 aur run2 ke beech sirf prompt badla.
- Temperature 0: AI har baar lagbhag same jawab deta hai. Repeatable test ke liye zaruri.
- Expected summary: sahi jawab, SIRF marks dene ke liye. Real app ise kabhi nahi dekhta.
- Planted error: jaan-bujhkar daali hui galti (galat medicine naam ya dose), ye dekhne ke
  liye ki checker pakadta hai ya nahi.
- Flag: "doctor, ise dekh lo". Flag khud kuch decide nahi karta. Doctor decide karta hai.
- Audit log: jo kuch hua uski list, time ke saath.
- Median: beech ka number. Ek bahut slow insaan result kharab nahi karta.
- Gate: ek number jo decide karta hai ki aage badhna hai ya nahi.


8. LOG YE SAWAAL POOCH SAKTE HAIN (imaandaar jawab)
------------------------------------------------
Q: T1 sirf 16 of 20 kyun hai?
A: 4 transcripts mein summary kuch history, symptom ya frequency chhod deti hai (T11, T12,
   T18, T19). Plan ke hisaab se 16 ya 17 "inconclusive" hai: theek karo aur ek baar run karo.

Q: Kya prompt sirf in 20 transcripts ke liye tune kiya gaya hai?
A: Haan, in 20 ki failures padhkar prompt sudhara. Naye transcripts par score kam ho sakta hai.

Q: Medicine list kahan se aayi?
A: General knowledge se likhi aur public drug labels aur sites se check ki. Kisi doctor ya
   pharmacist ne review nahi ki. Real product ke liye verified drug database chahiye.

Q: Checker kya NAHI pakadta?
A: Sahi naam + sahi dose, lekin is patient ke liye galat medicine. Wo ek tablet ki strength
   check karta hai, din ka total dose nahi.

Q: Card test (S9) itni aasani se pass kyun hota hai?
A: Database usi expected summaries se bana hai, to ye sirf lookup ka kaam dikhata hai.
   AI summary kitni achhi hai, ye T1 batata hai.

Q: 3 hi log kyun?
A: Plan ka number 3 hai. Ye pehla sign hai, saboot nahi. Aur ek role-player (R1) tool banane
   wala hai, isliye uske times shayad real doctors se kam hain.
