In simple words
---------------
We want to know: does the recall card really save the doctor's time?
So three people pretend to be doctors. We time how long they need to find the
key details of a patient's last visit:
  (a) with the recall card, and
  (b) by reading the full transcript.
We also time how long they need to review and approve a summary.
The numbers go in a file. A small script then says PASS or FAIL.

How a session works (a simple script for you)
---------------------------------------------
Before: python db/seed.py   (fresh database), then streamlit run app/app.py.
Each role-player gets:
  Task A (card):       "Open the patient X. Tell me the date, main complaint,
                        medicines with doses, and follow-up advice of the last
                        visit." Stopwatch stops when all 4 are said.
  Task B (transcript): the same question, but the person reads the full
                        transcript text file of a patient. Stopwatch stops when
                        all 4 are said.
  Task C (approval):   "Review this summary and approve it." Stopwatch starts
                        when the Review page is open and stops at Approve.
After each task you mark if the answer was correct. A wrong answer is saved,
but it is not counted in the median (it is counted in a separate "wrong" number).

How to be fair (very important)
-------------------------------
- One person must not do Task A and Task B on the same patient: the second time
  he or she already knows the answer. So each person gets different patients
  for A and for B.
- Everyone gets the same instructions, read aloud.
- A short practice round (not counted) before the real one.

What this test cannot tell us (we will say it in the results note)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- 3 role-players are very few. The medians are a first sign, not a proof.
- Role-players are not real doctors with real patients.
- The patients are fictional and the cards are clean, so real clinics may be
  slower.
- The card shows the old visit made from the expected summary, so the card is
  correct by construction. This test measures speed, not truth.

What can fail, and what we do
-----------------------------
- Median card time is 15 seconds or more: look at what slows people (many
  lines, small text, a hard to find box) and change only the screen.
- The card is less than 2x faster: the hypothesis of the plan is wrong
  ("we are wrong if the card is less than 2x faster"). We report it as it is.
- Approval takes 30 seconds or more: look at the Review page, maybe too many
  boxes before the Approve button.
- We never change the numbers after the sessions. Plan rule: "Mark Pass/Fail
  only after running. Never record a target as a result."
