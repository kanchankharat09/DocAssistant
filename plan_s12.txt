What the plan says

Story:  As the founder, I want a short results note and a review with Hitesh, so we

In simple words
---------------
One page that answers: "Did the idea work? Do we go on, fix something, or stop?"
It uses only numbers that we measured. Nothing is written that was not measured.

What goes in the note (one page)
--------------------------------
  1. Title, date, who wrote it, which build (git commit).
  2. A table of the five tests: target, result, Pass or Fail
        T1 Summary accuracy      need 18 of 20     result ...
        T2 Medicine-dose safety  need 10 of 10     result ...
        T3 Recall card           need 20 of 20     result ...
        T4 Review time           need under 30 s   result ... (or NOT MEASURED)
        T5 Hindi-English         need 4 of 5       result ...
     plus the speed test of S10: card median, transcript median, how many times faster.
  3. The call: GO, FIX or STOP, in one line, with the reason.
  4. What we found (3 to 5 short lines): what worked, what failed, what surprised us.
  5. Limits (3 to 5 short lines, honest):
       - 20 mock transcripts, 3 role-players, no real patients
       - the prompt was tuned on the same 20 transcripts (the score may be lower on new ones)
       - the medicine list is a demo list from public labels, not checked by a doctor
       - T1 words rules (frequency, symptom, history) are simple
  6. Next step if GO or FIX (2 or 3 lines).

How the call is made (from the hypothesis in step.md; Hitesh must agree)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  We believe it works if: T1 is at least 18 of 20, and the card median is under 15 s
  and at least 2x faster than the transcript.
  We are wrong (STOP) if: T1 is below 16 of 20 after two prompt iterations, or any wrong
  dose is saved as approved without a flag, or the card is less than 2x faster.
  16 or 17 of 20 on T1 is inconclusive (FIX): fix and re-run once.
  My proposal for the mapping:
     GO   all five tests Pass and the card is at least 2x faster
     FIX  T1 is 16 or 17, or one or two tests fail but no "we are wrong" rule is true
     STOP a "we are wrong" rule is true
  This mapping is my reading of the rule. We ask Hitesh in the review if he agrees.
